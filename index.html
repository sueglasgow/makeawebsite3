A few familiar faces

Who’s floating by?

Cloud names are clues: they describe what a cloud looks like or where it hangs out.

☁

Low & fluffy

Cumulus

Bright, puffy clouds with flat bottoms. They often show up on fair-weather days.

〰

High & wispy

Cirrus

Delicate streaks of ice crystals, brushed across the upper sky like a paintbrush.

☰

Low & layered

Stratus

A smooth gray blanket that can cover the sky and sometimes bring a little drizzle.

A pocket-sized sky fact

Every cloud has a story.

Clouds form when water vapor cools and gathers into tiny water droplets or ice crystals.
